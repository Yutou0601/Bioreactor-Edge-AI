# ICEA 投稿｜題目與摘要（定版 2026-07-27）

## Title
**A Multi-Signal Gas Utilization Prediction Model for Hydrogenotrophic Methanogens Under Pressure: Toward Physical–Biological Separation**

（中譯：微正壓下嗜氫甲烷菌之多訊號氣體利用預測模型——邁向物理／生物分離）

---

## Abstract (English)

Biological upgrading of carbon dioxide to methane by hydrogenotrophic methanogens
(CO₂ + 4H₂ → CH₄ + 2H₂O) under micro-positive-pressure recirculation is a promising
route to biogas valorization, yet its conversion efficiency is governed by a biological
gas-utilization rate that cannot be observed directly: the headspace pressure decline
reflects the **sum** of physical CO₂ dissolution and biological consumption, and the only
trustworthy edge signals are pressure, oxidation–reduction potential (ORP) and pH. We
present an edge-computing platform (Jetson Orin Nano) that ingests these signals at
one-minute resolution together with a knowledge-driven, multi-signal model of gas
utilization. Each automatic gas-refill cycle is treated as a relaxation experiment; a
first-order mass-transfer model P(t)=P_eq+(P₀−P_eq)e^(−k_La·t) predicts the pressure
trajectory with R²≈0.99 on a one-minute-circulation batch (six cycles over five days).
We prove, however, that in steady state the biological rate is **structurally
non-identifiable** from pressure alone—a constant biological sink is absorbed into the
apparent saturation pressure—so a good fit is not evidence of separation. We therefore
introduce two complementary separation routes: (i) a **multi-signal** route in which ORP
tracks hydrogen depletion (a biology-specific channel) and, via 1:4 stoichiometry,
constrains the biological fraction; and (ii) a **circulation-time** route that varies the
mass-transfer coefficient k_La (1/5/10 min) while biology is held constant, rendering the
physical and biological parameters jointly identifiable. On the present data the total
gas-utilization rate declines monotonically across the batch (r=−0.98, permutation
p=0.003) and the ORP recovery rate—an index of hydrogenotrophic activity—declines in
parallel (suggestive, permutation p=0.058), while pH is buffer-limited. Results are
reported with distribution-free permutation tests and cluster-robust inference, and the
transient and abiotic controls required to complete quantitative separation are outlined.
The contribution is an **identifiability-aware, multi-signal edge framework** that predicts
gas utilization now and provides a rigorous, deployable path toward physical–biological
separation.

## Keywords
hydrogenotrophic methanogenesis; biological biogas upgrading; gas–liquid mass transfer
(k_La); structural identifiability; oxidation–reduction potential (ORP); multi-signal
sensor fusion; edge computing; micro-positive-pressure bioreactor

## 中文摘要（內部用）

以嗜氫甲烷菌在微正壓循環下將二氧化碳轉為甲烷（CO₂+4H₂→CH₄+2H₂O），其轉換效率取決於
無法直接觀測的**生物氣體利用速率**：頭空壓力下降是「物理溶解＋生物消耗」之**總和**，而
可信的邊緣訊號僅有壓力、ORP 與 pH。本研究提出一套邊緣運算平台（Jetson Orin Nano），
每分鐘擷取上述訊號，並建立知識驅動之**多訊號**氣體利用模型。將每次自動補氣視為一次弛豫
實驗，以一階質傳模型 P(t)=P_eq+(P₀−P_eq)e^(−k_La·t) 預測壓力軌跡，於 1min 循環批次
（五日六循環）達 R²≈0.99。然而我們**證明**：穩態下生物速率由壓力**結構上不可辨識**
（常數生物匯被吸入表觀飽和壓），故擬合良好不等於已分離。為此提出兩條互補之分離路徑：
(i) **多訊號路徑**——ORP 追蹤氫氣消耗（生物專一通道），結合 1:4 化學計量約束生物份額；
(ii) **循環時間路徑**——改變質傳係數 k_La（1／5／10 分）而生物不變，使物理與生物參數
可聯合辨識。現有資料顯示總氣體利用率隨批次單調下降（r=−0.98，置換 p=0.003），代表嗜氫
活性之 ORP 回升率亦同步下降（暗示，置換 p=0.058），pH 則受緩衝限制。所有結果以無分布
假設之置換檢定與叢集穩健推論呈現，並列出完成量化分離所需之暫態與無菌對照。本研究貢獻在於
一套**具可辨識性意識之多訊號邊緣框架**：當下即可預測氣體利用，並提供邁向物理／生物分離之
嚴謹且可部署路徑。
